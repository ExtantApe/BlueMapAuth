First version, for Paper 26.1 and up.

- Serves BlueMap's web map behind a sign-in, with a one-time link from Latchkey's "Open the map".
- Shows each viewer only the worlds they may enter and the players they may see. Anything else is "not found".
- Fails closed: without HouseRules or LuckPerms, it shows less, never more.

Tested: 24 HTTP and config tests, a bot test with the real BlueMap, LuckPerms, HouseRules and Latchkey on Paper 26.1.2, and the hand-off page in a real browser against made-up data. On 26.2 and 26.3 it has only been loaded, switched off. Not yet tried behind Caddy.
